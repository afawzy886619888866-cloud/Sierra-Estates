## Brand & Style

The design system combines high-conviction luxury real estate portfolio governance with the precision and tactical telemetry of an aerospace diagnostic command center. Crafted for high-net-worth investors, asset managers, and elite brokers, the interface transforms property acquisition, asset telemetry, smart-home environmental analytics, and capital flow into an ultra-premium, heads-up control terminal.

The aesthetic fuses high-contrast telemetry styling with deep obsidian dark glassmorphism and surgical precision:
- **Obsidian Telemetry Foundations:** Layered deep abyssal blue-black glass surfaces evoke high-security terminal architectures, removing visual clutter and putting live property intelligence front and center.
- **Luminous Cybernetic Accents:** Laser-crisp cyan (`#00E5FF`) and orbital electric blue (`#0077FF`) delineate vital status channels and telemetry bounds, balanced by warm amber gold (`#F59E0B`) to indicate ultra-luxury assets, yields, escrow states, and executive alerts.
- **Tactical Real Estate Cockpit:** HUD-styled bracketed enclosures, linear diagnostic levelers, and numeric data badges deliver an elite, military-grade financial intelligence environment.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep dark mode, relying on extreme chromatic contrast between ultra-dense midnight surfaces and photon-emitting accent paths:

- **Primary (`#00E5FF`):** Cyan Luminescence. Serves as the primary operational hue, highlighting live telemetry lines, primary active states, focal metric counters, and focused data rings.
- **Secondary (`#0077FF`):** Deep Orbital Electric Blue. Used for foundational borders, secondary structural data paths, selected navigation surfaces, and subdued metric trajectories.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Imperial Gold Neon. Reserved for luxury portfolio designations, capital valuations, pending escrows, mission-critical notifications, and tier-one estate badges.
- **Neutral Foundations (`#070B19` base, `#0D1527` container, `#131D36` elevated deck):** Subdued obsidian and midnight navy backdrops that absorb ambient light, providing deep contrast for neon strokes without visual fatigue.
- **Text & Foreground Spectrum:**
  - Foreground Primary: `#F0F6FC` (Cold surgical white for maximum crispness)
  - Foreground Secondary: `#8BA2C4` (Muted cyan-slate for auxiliary metadata and axis labels)
  - Foreground Tertiary: `#4B6185` (Deep atmospheric blue-grey for inactive states and grid lines)

## Typography

The typographical architecture leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, structural headlines, and editorial narrative copy, providing modern geometric clarity, exceptional legibility, and premium luxury poise.

For telemetry readouts, status identifiers, metric tickers, coordinates, and HUD diagnostic labels, **Space Grotesk** is deployed. Its calibrated technical character, monospace-leaning cadence, and structured capitals supply authentic command-center legibility without sacrificing editorial sophistication. All small labels utilize uppercase transformation with expanded letter-spacing to reinforce tactical instrumentation clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The viewport architecture follows a high-density, modular command cockpit model built on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (adapting to 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile). 

- **Grid Alignment:** Spacing adheres strictly to an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 36px).
- **Desktop Architecture:** The layout accommodates an integrated tactical sidebar (260px fixed width) paired with a fluid multi-widget dashboard canvas. Cards are organized into diagnostic clusters (e.g., HVAC/IoT status arrays, portfolio cap-rate gauges, satellite map plots, live valuation feeds).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Collapses into a vertical telemetry stream. HUD gauges adapt from multi-dial rows into stacked gauge widgets. Edge margins compress to `1rem` to maximize spatial yield for diagnostic cards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Modular 2-column card split with collapsed micro-icon drawer navigation.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full panoramic command console, supporting side-by-side estate telemetry panels and dynamic multi-metric data feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft natural drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal obsidian layering, multi-stop neon hairline borders, and localized ambient photonic glows**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Base background tint `#070B19` with a subtle 24px isometric vector grid pattern at 4% cyan opacity.
- **Tier 1 (Command Decks & Data Cells):** Surface fill `#0D1527` with 90% opacity, frosted backdrop-filter (`blur(16px)`), framed by a 1px border of `#0077FF` at 30% opacity.
- **Tier 2 (Active/Critical Diagnostic Cards):** Surface fill `#131D36` framed by a dual-line neon stroke (`#00E5FF` at 80% opacity) coupled with an exterior luminescent aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -3px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25), inset 0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating HUD Overlays & Modals):** Semi-opaque `#070B19` at 95% opacity with high-index cyan and amber edge highlights, accompanied by 32px radial blur haloing to simulate glowing glass displays hovering above the console deck.
- **HUD Accent Borders:** Selected cards feature asymmetrical neon corner-brackets or a dual-tone edge (e.g., cyan primary perimeter with an amber right-flange status tab) that mirrors the aesthetic of avionics hardware.

## Shapes

The interface balances sleek cybernetic styling with contemporary luxury software design using `roundedness: 2`:

- **Card Enclosures & Modules:** Built with `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) or `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) bounding perimeters, preserving tactical structure while softening abrasive brutalist angles.
- **Diagnostic Badges & Gauges:** Pills (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for operational chips (e.g., `ONLINE`, `OFF-MARKET`, `HEATING ACTIVE`, `SETTLED`).
- **Telemetry Framing Corners:** Strategic HUD cards feature micro chamfers (45-degree angled edge caps) or outer rounded perimeters accented with inset rectangular data notches.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Cyber Action:** Background of `#00E5FF`, label colored `#070B19` in `Space Grotesk` (bold). Hover triggers a pulsing photon halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a 1px `#0077FF` border, white text, and an inner cyan glow on hover.
- **Tertiary Amber Action:** High-stakes operations (e.g., "EXECUTE ESCROW WIRE", "LOCK SYSTEM") utilize `#F59E0B` borders and amber neon backlighting.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped containers with 1px luminous stroke. Contains a pulsing 6px beacon dot:
  - Active / Normal: `#00E5FF` glow dot on deep slate-cyan container.
  - Escrow / Caution / Premium: `#F59E0B` gold glow dot on deep gold-navy container.
  - Alert / Compromised: `#EF4444` crimson glow dot.

### Telemetry Cards & Metric Panels
- Deep midnight containers (`#0D1527`) with 1px border lines shifting from `#0077FF` to `#00E5FF`.
- Headers feature uppercase `Space Grotesk` labels accompanied by subtle geometric iconography (crosshairs, hex nodes, or bracket indicators).
- Footers integrate live sparklines, radial HUD progress gauges, or dual-bar resource levelers mimicking server and diagnostic performance panels.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Filters
- Input fields use `#070B19` inset backgrounds with 1px border `#1E2D4A`. 
- Active state transitions immediately to a `#00E5FF` border with a subtle 8px perimeter glow.
- Monospaced numeric alignment for financial figures, square-footage data, and tokenized smart contract values.

### HUD Gauges & Graphs
- **Circular Diagnostic Dial:** Ring track in `#131D36`, active gauge arc rendered with glowing cyan or amber neon gradients, center displaying `telemetry-metric` font.
- **Frequency Waveforms & Sparklines:** Crisp 1.5px vector strokes in `#00E5FF` over low-opacity gradient area fills (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)` to zero).
- **Linear Telemetry Bars:** High-tech stepped segmentation markers showing asset occupancy, energy storage, and escrow completion progress.
