## 4. Investor narrative

### The opportunity

Sierra Estates can offer investors a structured way to access real-estate inventory while reducing the friction that commonly exists between fragmented listings, incomplete availability data, inconsistent pricing, and slow follow-up. The proposed operating model combines inventory management, investment analysis, marketing distribution, broker activation, and CRM reporting into one commercial workflow.

### The commercial engine

The operating plan is strongest when shown as a repeatable funnel: verified inventory is packaged into clear listings; demand is generated through digital campaigns and broker relationships; interested prospects move to one defined action, such as a lead form, WhatsApp conversation, or scheduled viewing; and every lead is tracked through the CRM until reservation, contract, or disqualification.

### The execution plan

The supplied operating plan proposes preparation, launch, intensification, and closing/evaluation stages. The investor deck should translate this into measurable gates: inventory confirmation, creative approval, campaign launch, qualified lead volume, viewing attendance, reservation rate, contract conversion, and return on marketing spend.

## 6. Facebook and Instagram campaign architecture

### Campaign objective

The first campaign should generate qualified inquiries and scheduled viewings rather than optimize for broad engagement alone. Each advertisement should lead to one action: **request the updated inventory**, **book a viewing**, or **request the investor pack**.

Because housing and real-estate advertising can be subject to platform-specific restrictions and special-category requirements, the campaign setup and audience options must be checked inside Meta Ads Manager before launch. Do not use discriminatory or exclusionary targeting language.

| Funnel stage | Campaign role | Recommended creative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Introduce the project and brand | Logo animation, project identity, location/value proposition, short vertical video |
| Consideration | Explain the opportunity | Carousel of unit types, tower/floor/area highlights, payment-plan explainer |
| Lead generation | Capture intent | Instant form or landing page with unit preference and viewing request |
| Remarketing | Recover undecided prospects | Availability update, viewing reminder, deadline-based offer only after verification |
| Investor outreach | Reach multi-unit prospects | Investor-pack ad emphasizing organized data, priority selection, and private briefing |

### Suggested ad concepts

**Concept A — “Find the right unit, not just an advertisement.”**  
Primary text: “Compare available administrative and commercial units with clear areas, pricing, and payment options. Request the latest Sierra Estates inventory and arrange a professional viewing.”  
Headline: “Request the updated inventory”  
Call to action: “Learn more” or “Get quote.”

**Concept B — “Ready-to-deliver opportunities.”**  
Primary text: “Looking for a unit that can move from decision to possession quickly? Ask Sierra Estates for verified availability, current terms, and a scheduled site visit.”  
Headline: “Book a project viewing”  
Call to action: “Sign up.”

**Concept C — “Investor pack for serious buyers.”**  
Primary text: “Considering more than one unit? Request the Sierra Estates investor pack with inventory, pricing, payment structures, and an acquisition discussion tailored to your objectives.”  
Headline: “Request the investor pack”  
Call to action: “Contact us.”

**Concept D — “A structured route from inquiry to acquisition.”**  
Primary text: “From current availability to viewing and reservation, Sierra Estates organizes the next step clearly. Submit your preferred unit type and our team will contact you.”  
Headline: “Start with a clear plan”  
Call to action: “Send message.”

### Lead form fields

Use a short form to protect lead quality and completion rate: full name, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred unit type, approximate budget range, preferred purchase purpose, preferred viewing date, and consent to be contacted. Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive personal data.

### Creative system

Use the supplied Sierra Estates logo as the visual anchor. Maintain a restrained palette based on the logo: deep navy, cool blue, light grey, and white. Use **Sierra Estates** as the brand and **Cairo Plaza** as the project name across every placement. Keep the same price format, area unit, contact number, and call-to-action across all placements. Produce vertical 9:16 versions for Stories and Reels, 4:5 versions for Feed, and square versions only where needed for catalog-style use.

### Measurement plan

Track the funnel using standardized, source-defined metrics in Meta Ads Manager. At minimum, review Reach, Impressions, Link clicks, Leads, Messaging conversations started, Amount spent, CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions), CPC (all), and Cost per result where available. Do not combine results from campaigns with different objectives into one cost-per-result figure.
