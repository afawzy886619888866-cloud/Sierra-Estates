## Detailed Compound Profiles & Positioning

### 1. Emaar Projects
- **Uptown Cairo**: Highest prestige, Mokattam hills elevation, golf & pool views, strict gate security (The Fourteen gate), premier expat preference. Typical 3BR: 65k–100k EGP / $1,500–$2,200.
- **Mivida**: Prime 5th Settlement location on Southern 90th St, parcels 1–33, exceptionally high rental liquidity and quick turnover. Typical 3BR: 48k–70k EGP.

### 2. SODIC Projects
- **Villette & Sky Condos**: Contemporary architectural layouts, extensive central green spine, premium clubhouse facilities. Typical 3BR: 45k–65k EGP.
- **Eastown**: Directly on Southern 90th Street adjacent to AUC; maximum walkability to dining, banks, and corporate hubs. Typical 3BR: 45k–75k EGP.

### 3. Family, Value & Detached Communities
- **Madinaty**: Comprehensive self-contained city, ideal for families seeking standalone detached villas with private gardens and pools. Detached villas: 70k–140k EGP.
- **Fifth Square (Al Marasem)**: Modern delivery with ACs & kitchens included, Moon Residence zone. Typical 3BR: 35k–50k EGP.
- **Al Shorouk Springs / El Patio**: Quiet, spacious standalone villa and townhouse options close to international schools and Badr City.
- **Cairo Festival City (CFC)**: Prime North 90th St location, walkability to CFC Mall, preferred destination for corporate and diplomatic tenancies.
