# Real Estate Objection Handling & Sierra Blue Policies

## 1. Upfront & Advance Payments

- **Landlords' Request:** 6 to 12 months upfront.
- **Strategy:** Frame advance payments as leverage for a 15%–25% discount. Offer quarterly payments with adjusted rates if needed.

## 2. Portals Price Discrepancies (Property Finder / Bayut)

- **Context:** Clients frequently find conflicting lower prices on online ads.
- **Strategy:** Clarify that many online ads are outdated or broker clickbait. Sierra verifies directly with vetted property owners.

## 3. Semi-Furnished vs. Fully Furnished

- **Strategy:** On leases of 2+ years, semi-furnished units (Kitchen + ACs) save 500k–1M EGP overall, allowing clients to own their furniture assets.

## 4. Diplomatic & Corporate Leases

- **Diplomatic Tenants:** 30–60 days early exit notice without penalty upon official transfer.
- **Corporate Entities:** Require commercial registration, tax card, authorization letter, and direct bank transfers.
