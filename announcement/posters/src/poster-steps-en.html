Official Announcement

Booking & Contracting Steps
Cairo Plaza El-Mataria Project

◆
1
The client is entitled to review the project documents and the company's papers (building licence – engineering names of floors – contract template – commercial register).
2
A booking form is completed for applicants and a deposit of up to EGP 5,000 (five thousand Egyptian pounds) is paid at the Marketing Office located on the Cairo Plaza El-Mataria project site; the client is entitled to a copy of the form as proof.
3
The contract is signed; the original booking form stamped with the company seal is received, the contracting deposit is paid, and payment receipts are received from the Financial Administration in Building (1), second floor, Cairo Plaza El-Mataria.
4
The company-signed original contract is received within a maximum of (7) business days from the client's signing date.
